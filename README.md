# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard


Overview
This project analyzes the Superstore dataset to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and product categories. Using Python, Pandas, and Plotly, an interactive dashboard was created to visualize key business metrics and support data-driven decision-making.
The project covers data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business performance analysis, and interactive data visualization.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were:
Analyze monthly sales trends and identify peak and low-performing months.
Evaluate sales performance across product categories and sub-categories.
Analyze profitability trends and identify the most profitable business areas.
Compare sales and profit contribution across customer segments.
Calculate sales-to-profit ratios for business performance evaluation.
Build interactive visualizations to communicate business insights effectively.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 1. Monthly Sales Analysis
Highest Sales Month: December ($325.29K)
Lowest Sales Month: January ($94.92K)
The analysis indicates strong seasonal demand during the holiday season, resulting in significantly higher sales in December.

### 2. Sales Analysis by Category
Highest Sales Category: Technology ($836.15K)
Lowest Sales Category: Furniture ($742.00K)
Technology products generated the highest revenue among all product categories.

### 3. Sales Analysis by Sub-Category
Highest Sales Sub-Category: Phones ($330.01K)
Second Highest: Chairs ($328.50K)
Lowest Sales Sub-Category: Fasteners ($3.02K)
Phones and Chairs were the major contributors to overall revenue.

### 4. Monthly Profit Analysis
Highest Profit Month: December ($43.37K)
Lowest Profit Month: January ($9.13K)
Profit trends closely followed sales trends, with December emerging as the most profitable month.

### 5. Profit Analysis by Category and Sub-Category
Most Profitable Category: Technology ($145.45K)
Most Profitable Sub-Category: Copiers ($55.62K)
Technology products demonstrated strong profit margins and business value.
